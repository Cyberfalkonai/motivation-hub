The Obstacle in Our Path

In ancient times, a king had a boulder placed directly on a roadway. He then hid himself nearby to watch and see if anyone would move the massive stone out of the way.

Some of the king's wealthiest merchants and courtiers passed by. Instead of moving it, they simply walked around it. Many loudly blamed the king for not keeping the roads clear, but none of them did anything to solve the problem.

Eventually, a peasant came along carrying a heavy load of vegetables. Upon approaching the boulder, the peasant laid down his burden and tried to push the stone out of the road. After much straining and hard work, he finally succeeded.

As the peasant picked up his vegetables, he noticed a purse lying on the road where the boulder had been. The purse contained many gold coins and a note from the king explaining that the gold was for the person who removed the obstacle from the roadway.

Lesson of the Story

  • Complaining about a bad situation does absolutely nothing to fix it.
  • Every obstacle we encounter presents a hidden opportunity to improve our circumstances.
  • Those who take direct initiative are the ones who reap life's greatest rewards.
  • Hard work and persistence unlock blessings that lazy bypassers will never find.
  • Do not wait for others or the government to clear your path — take ownership and do it yourself.
  • True leaders look for practical solutions while others waste time pointing fingers and placing blame.

Main Message or Lesson of Story

OBSTACLES ARE MERELY DISGUISED OPPORTUNITIES FOR GROWTH, PROGRESS, AND REWARD. BY FACING THEM HEAD-ON WITH RESOLUTE INITIATIVE AND DETERMINATION, YOU DISCOVER HIDDEN TREASURES AND STRENGTHS THAT OTHERS MISS BY TRYING TO WALK AROUND THE PROBLEM.
