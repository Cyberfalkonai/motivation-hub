A farmer had an old donkey that accidentally fell into the deep well. The farmer tried to think of a way to rescue it, but the well was deep and the donkey was old. He decided it wasn't worth the effort, so he asked his neighbors to help him fill the well with dirt.

As the first shovelfuls of dirt landed on the donkey, it cried loudly. Then something unexpected happened. Every time dirt fell on its back, the donkey shook it off and stepped up. More dirt came, and each time the donkey shook it off and stepped higher.

Eventually, after many shovelfuls of dirt, the donkey reached the top of the well and walked away safely.

The Lesson of the Story

  • Life will sometimes throw problems, failures, and disappointment at you.
  • Don't let those difficulties bury you.
  • Instead, shake them off and use them as steps to move higher.
  • Persistence and a positive attitude can turn obstacles into opportunities.
  • Life is a set of unending changes — so you must be able to survive among the different changes that may occur.
  • "DON'T STAY DOWN. SHAKE OFF YOUR PROBLEMS AND STEP UP" this will lead you to one of the most successful men in your community.
  • Turn problems into opportunities. What seems like the end can become a new beginning if you respond wisely.
  • Never give up. Difficult situations do not have to determine your future.
  • Shake off negativity. Just as the donkey shook off each shovel of dirt, don't let criticism, failure or disappointment that bury you.
  • Use every obstacle as stepping stone. The dirt meant to bury the donkey became the steps it used to climb out.
  • Stay calm during difficulties. Panicking rarely solves problems; clear thinking helps you find a way forward.
  • Persistence leads to success. The donkey kept shaking off the dirt and stepping up until it reached the top.
  • Focus on solutions, not problems. The donkey didn't waste energy complaining, it kept moving upward.
  • Every set back can prepare you for come back. Failure is not always the end, it can become part of your success.
  • Resilience is the key to success. The ability to recover from difficulties is one of life's greatest strengths.

Main Message

NO MATTER HOW DIFFICULT YOUR SITUATION IS, DON'T STAY DOWN. SHAKE OFF EVERY CHALLENGE, LEARN FROM IT, AND USE IT AS A STEP TOWARD SUCCESS. OBSTACLES DO NOT HAVE TO BURY YOU — THEY CAN BECOME THE FOUNDATION THAT LIFTS YOU HIGHER IF YOU RESPOND WITH COURAGE, PERSEVERANCE, AND A POSITIVE ATTITUDE.
