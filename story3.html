RISING WATER

Here's the story: A man lived in a town where heavy rain caused floodwaters to rise. The authorities warned everyone to evacuate, but the man said: "I have faith, God will save me."

As the water rose, his neighbor came by in a car and offered to take him to safety. The man refused.

Later, the water reached his house. A boat came to rescue him, but he said: "No, thank you. God will save me."

The flood kept rising until he climbed onto the roof. A helicopter arrived and lowered a rope ladder, but he still refused.

Finally, the flood water covered the house, and the man drowned.

When he reached heaven, he asked God, "Lord, why didn't you save me?"

God replied: "I sent you a warning, a car, a boat, and a helicopter. What more were you waiting for?"

Lesson of the Story

  • Don't ignore any opportunities because they don't look the way you expected.
  • Faith and action should work together.
  • Success often comes through people, opportunities, and wise decisions — not always through dramatic miracles.
  • Take responsibility for your choices — your decision has consequences.
  • Listen to wise advice — warning and guidance can save you from trouble.
  • Don't be stubborn — being unwilling to change your mind can lead to failure.
  • Be open-minded — don't reject help simply because it doesn't match your expectations.
  • Use the resources available to you — people, knowledge, and opportunities are often tools to help you succeed.
  • Act at the right time — delaying action can cause you to miss your chance.
  • Success requires cooperation — accepting help from others is a strength, not a weakness.

Main Message or Lesson of Story

GOD (OR LIFE) OFTEN ANSWERS OUR PRAYERS BY PROVIDING OPPORTUNITIES, PEOPLE, AND RESOURCES. WE MUST RECOGNIZE THEM AND TAKE ACTION INSTEAD OF WAITING FOR A MIRACLE WHILE DOING NOTHING.
